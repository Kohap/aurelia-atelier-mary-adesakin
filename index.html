The Artist

Adesakin Mary Damilola

Born in Ile-Ife, Osun State, Mary is a Nigerian thread painter and textile artist whose work stitches together emotion, memory, Yoruba heritage, and contemporary life.

Education

Fine and Applied Arts, Adeyemi Federal University of Education, Ondo.

Practice

Thread painting, textile design, fabric dyeing, portraiture, and hand-built surface narratives.

Base

Ile-Ife / Modakeke, Osun State, Nigeria, with recent exhibition activity in Nigeria and Paris.

Languages

English and Yoruba.

Artist Statement

Mary uses thread as both material and metaphor: a line that repairs, remembers, conceals, reveals, and connects.

Her works transform textile gestures into portraits of interior life. Across pieces such as Loud Silence, Visible Within, and Maze of Uncertainty, stitched surfaces become a way to speak about vulnerability, resilience, justice, femininity, and cultural inheritance.

Material Language

Thread, canvas, jute, pigment, and hand-built texture.

Core Themes

Identity, memory, emotion, womanhood, justice, self-love, Yoruba heritage, and everyday experience.

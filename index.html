The Artist

Adesakin Mary Damilola

Born in Ile-Ife, Osun State, Mary is a Nigerian thread painter and textile artist whose work stitches together emotion, memory, Yoruba heritage, and contemporary life.

Education

Fine and Applied Arts, Adeyemi Federal University of Education, Ondo.

Practice

Thread painting, textile design, fabric dyeing, portraiture, and hand-built surface narratives.

Base

Ile-Ife, Osun State, Nigeria, with recent exhibition activity in Nigeria and Paris.

Languages

English and Yoruba.

Artist Statement

I use thread as both material and metaphor: a line that repairs, remembers, conceals, reveals, and connects.

My works transform textile gestures into portraits of interior life. Across pieces such as Loud Silence, Visible Within, and Maze of Uncertainty, I use stitched surfaces to speak about vulnerability, resilience, justice, femininity, and cultural inheritance.

Material Language

Thread, canvas, jute, pigment, and hand-built texture.

Core Themes

Identity, memory, emotion, womanhood, justice, self-love, Yoruba heritage, and everyday experience.

Commissions

Commission a Thread Portrait or Textile Work

Mary accepts select commissions for collectors, curators, and institutions interested in symbolic thread-based portraits, narrative textile works, and emotionally grounded original pieces.

1. Conversation

Tell the Story

Share the person, memory, theme, or emotion the work should hold. Reference images and symbolic details are welcome.

2. Direction

Confirm Scope

Mary confirms size, surface, medium, timeline, and quotation before work begins.

3. Studio Work

Stitched Slowly

Each piece is handmade with thread, pigment, and textile gestures, allowing the work to develop with care.

4. Delivery

Invoice & Shipping

A formal invoice and shipping quote are prepared once details are confirmed with the collector.

Who are commissions for?

Collectors, families, galleries, curators, and institutions seeking original thread-based works with personal or cultural meaning.

How is pricing handled?

Pricing depends on size, surface, complexity, materials, and delivery destination. Mary confirms a quote before beginning.

Can collectors outside Nigeria inquire?

Yes. Communication can happen by email, with shipping quoted according to artwork size and destination.
